LinkRing

Privacy Policy · Last updated: 25 November 2026

LinkRing is a puzzle game for iPhone. This policy explains what the app stores, what it sends, and what it does not do. It is written to describe this app specifically rather than to cover things it does not contain.

The short version

LinkRing has no accounts, no sign-in, and no server of its own. Your scores and settings never leave your device. The only component that transmits anything is the advertising SDK, described below.

What is stored on your device

The game saves the following locally, using Apple's standard app preferences storage. It stays on your iPhone, is not transmitted anywhere, and is deleted when you delete the app:

  • your best score, separately for Normal and Hard
  • the game currently in progress, so it can be resumed
  • which difficulty you last played
  • your language, sound and haptics settings
  • which one-time hints and the tutorial you have already seen
  • a count of completed runs, used to keep the first few free of ads and to decide when to ask you to rate the app

None of this identifies you. There is no name, email address, contact, photo, location, or health data anywhere in the app.

Advertising

LinkRing shows ads through Unity Ads, operated by Unity Technologies. There are two places they can appear: an optional video you may choose to watch to continue a finished run, and an occasional full-screen ad between runs. The rewarded video is always your choice — the game never requires you to watch one.

The app requests non-personalized advertising. Unity is told not to use your activity to build an advertising profile, and the app does not request Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA), which is why you are never asked for tracking permission.

Unity Ads still receives technical information needed to deliver and measure an ad — such as device type and operating system, coarse region and language, IP address, a non-permanent device identifier, and whether an ad was shown or completed. This data is handled by Unity as an independent controller under their own policy, not by the developer of this app, and the developer never receives it:

  • Unity — Game Player and App User Privacy Policy

What the app does not do

  • No analytics. Unity Analytics, Cloud Diagnostics, crash reporting and performance reporting are all disabled in this build.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No accounts, login, or cloud save.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites, and no IDFA.
  • No data is sold or shared for anyone else's marketing.

Children

LinkRing is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. Because the app shows third-party ads, it is not suitable for Apple's Kids Category.

Your choices

Deleting the app removes everything it has stored on your device. For the advertising data described above, Unity's own policy sets out the choices and rights available to you, including how to contact them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated. Material changes will be reflected here before a new version of the app is released.
